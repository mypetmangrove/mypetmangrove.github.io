    body: |-
      Two things, a few miles apart.

      **On the shore,** every tide washes mangrove propagules ashore. Thousands of them. Most dry out and die.

      **In the classroom,** students learn about coastal ecosystems from slides and textbooks.
    callout: What if every one of those propagules became a student's responsibility?

    heading: Our founder
    subheading: Alex Webster, Founder and CEO
    lead: >-
      Alex Webster is an internationally recognized marine researcher, ocean advocate, and youth
      leader whose lifelong commitment to protecting coastal ecosystems was shaped by growing up
      along Florida's ever-degrading Indian River Lagoon.
    body: |-
      By age eight, Alex had logged over 150 volunteer hours dedicated to coastal preservation,
      emerging as a small, yet powerful, voice for sustainability. Through middle and high school,
      Alex researched human-made stressors in marine systems with Florida institutes and
      universities, and presented findings worldwide to inform Florida policy.

      The lesson: a great finding doesn't restore anything if nobody hears it.
    callout: >-
      Alex envisions a future where every student becomes both a scientist and a steward, proving
      that sustainability begins not in policy or theory, but in curiosity, care, and the simple act
      of planting a seed.
