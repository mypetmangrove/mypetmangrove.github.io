      - question: Which grades can take part?
        answer: Any grade, Pre-K through 12. The [lesson library](/teacher-resources/) is organized by grade so you can find activities that fit your students.

        answer: Most propagules are ready after 6 to 15 weeks growing in a jar of water in your classroom.
      - question: Where do the mangroves get planted?
        answer: Along degraded shorelines at local partner sites on the Indian River Lagoon, at the right elevation for the tides.
