  - type: text

    body: |-
      **What we collect.** When you use a form on this site, we keep what you type (your name, email,
      organization, and message) so we can reply. If you join our email list, we keep your email
      address until you ask us to remove it.

      **Teacher portal.** Teachers create an account with their name, school, and email. Class rosters
      hold whatever the teacher enters; we ask for first names and last initials only. Classes,
      seedlings, check-ins, and photos are visible only to the teacher who created them and to the My
      Pet Mangrove team. A photo appears on our public website only if the teacher marks it as okay to
      share and we approve it. We ask teachers to upload photos of seedlings, not of students.

      **What we don't do.** We don't sell or rent anyone's information, and we don't use it for
      advertising.

      **Where it's stored.** Form messages and portal data are stored with our database provider,
      Supabase. Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages. YouTube videos load only when you press play.

      **Removing your information.** Email [mypetmangrove@gmail.com](mailto:mypetmangrove@gmail.com)
      and we'll delete your messages, email sign-up, or teacher account. Teachers can delete students,
      seedlings, photos, and classes themselves at any time.
