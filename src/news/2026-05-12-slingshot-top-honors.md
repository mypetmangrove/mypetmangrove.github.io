On May 12, 2026, the National Geographic Society announced the Top Honors projects of the 2026 Slingshot Challenge. My Pet Mangrove was one of five, chosen from more than 2,300 video submissions by 5,900+ young people in 104 countries. Each Top Honors project received $10,000.

The other Top Honors projects were Homes for Hornbills (Thailand), Lighting Paradox in São Paulo (Brazil), Restoring Habitat with Smart Rock (United States), and S.O.S. Manatus (Colombia).

Our 60-second video, *You want WHAT for Christmas?*, was published by National Geographic Education. [Watch it on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dS_Z_FJvWJU).

The award goes toward more classrooms, a teacher kit any teacher can run on their own, and community planting days with our restoration partners.
